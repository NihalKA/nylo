HERE TO HELP

Need a hand?

Email admin@colorforfun.app.

Include your app version, iOS version, selected provider/model and a short description of the issue. Remove private details from screenshots. Never send your API key, account password or verification codes.

Connecting your AI

Open Settings → Connect your AI. Choose your provider, enter its API key, and select several available models, select all returned models, or enter a model ID manually. Choose one active model in each chat. Custom providers also need their HTTPS API base URL. Saving a connection does not prove model access; the first reply may identify an account or model restriction.

Replies stop when I leave

The first version works while open. Partial replies are saved; retry starts a new provider request and may incur another charge. Nylo cannot run scheduled work in the background.

Moving phones or deleting data

Export a backup from Settings before uninstalling or moving phones. Backups contain private readable text and attachments and exclude keys, which must be entered again after restoring. Delete individual chats, agents, memories and connections from their controls. Contact support for assistance; support cannot retrieve device-only chats.

Incorrect or harmful AI output

AI responses may be wrong. Do not rely on them alone for important decisions. Use Report response beside an answer to preview a report and open your email app. Including the selected response text is optional; no other chats, files or keys are included. Nothing is delivered until you send the email. You can also remove an unwanted response from your device. Contact support directly if no email app is configured.
