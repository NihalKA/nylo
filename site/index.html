YOUR OWN SPACE

Your conversations.
Your choice of AI.

A simple place to ask, think and write. Connect your own AI provider, keep your chats together, and create assistants with a memory of their own.

Nylo is being built for iPhone. Chats and agent memory are saved on your device. When you send a message, its context goes directly to the provider you choose.

Currently in development. Nylo is not yet available on the App Store. Your provider’s API charges apply; an API key is required for live replies. There is no automatic cloud sync.

Familiar conversations. Useful continuity.

Use Chats for everyday questions. Create an agent when you want dedicated instructions and saved memories across conversations. You can review, edit or remove those memories.
